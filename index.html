      ABOUT RED BULL

      Red Bull began as Krating Daeng, a drink developed in Thailand. A retailer listing describes the original formula as dating to 1976, and the brand was later built into an international product marketed by an Austrian company. An article citing Red Bull Japan states the blue-silver can was sold in 167 countries (2016 figure).

      Sources: arcasiangrocer.com.au; gigazine.net (2016, citing Red Bull Japan). Not a primary Red Bull source — verify at redbull.com before formal use.
