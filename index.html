ABOUT RED BULL

Red Bull Energy Drink launched on 1 April 1987 in Austria and created the caffeinated energy drink category. Known for its slim silver-and-blue can and the tagline “Red Bull gives you wiiings,” it is enjoyed by athletes, students, professionals and travelers.
